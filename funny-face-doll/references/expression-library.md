# Cartoon Expression Library (卡通表情库)

## 通用卡通表情（适用于所有角色）

### 1. 巨大卡通小孩头 (Giant Cartoon Child Head)
**适用场景**: 首次出现的卡通变形（Shot 2）
**中文**: 圆滚滚的大脸、油亮的黑色刘海、胖嘟嘟的红润脸颊、巨大的白色眼珠向上瞟、小巧撅起的嘴唇
**English**: an enormous round face with glossy dark bangs, chubby rosy cheeks, huge white eyes glancing upward, and small pouting lips
**变体A（更圆润）**: 眼珠向外突出，嘴巴呈小圆"O"型 / wide-set bulging eyes and a tiny round "o" mouth
**变体B（摇晃）**: 大头轻轻左右摇晃 / the oversized head sways gently

### 2. 拇指拉嘴宽笑 (Thumb-Stretched Grin)
**适用场景**: 经典搞笑表情（Shot 4, 6）
**中文**: 用两根拇指钩住嘴角两侧向外拉扯，将嘴巴撑成一个不可能的宽度咧嘴笑，露出牙齿，舌头伸出
**English**: hooking both thumbs into the corners of her/his mouth and pulling outward, stretching her/his mouth into an impossibly wide grin with teeth bared and tongue lolling out
**变体（带眼镜）**: 红框眼镜因脸部拉扯而微微歪斜 / her/his red-purple glasses tilting slightly from the facial distortion

### 3. 拉长窄脸 (Stretched Narrow Face)
**适用场景**: 夸张变形（Shot 7）
**中文**: 一张被拉长的窄脸，两只巨大的椭圆白色眼睛和一张微张的小嘴
**English**: a stretched, narrow face with two huge oval white eyes and a small open mouth

### 4. 捏脸颊微笑 (Cheek-Pinch Smile)
**适用场景**: 搞怪但可爱的表情（Shot 8）
**中文**: 用食指捏住两侧脸颊，将脸部挤压成一个宽幅闭眼微笑
**English**: pinching both cheeks with her/his index fingers, forcing her/his face into a wide closed-eye smile
**变体（带眼镜）**: 红框眼镜被推到鼻尖上方 / glasses pushed up above the bridge of her/his nose

### 5. 叉腰坏笑 (Hands-on-Hips Smirk)
**适用场景**: 真实面部恢复 + 调皮表情（Shot 9）
**中文**: 双手叉腰，带着调皮的坏笑向画面左侧瞥一眼
**English**: both hands planted on her/his hips, glancing toward the left of frame with a playful smirk

### 6. 陶醉大张 (Blissful Arms Wide)
**适用场景**: 冷静恢复时刻（Shot 10）
**中文**: 双臂大张，闭眼仰头一脸陶醉；手臂缓缓放下，恢复到初始姿势，对镜头露出温和的闭唇微笑
**English**: flinging both arms wide open, eyes closed and chin lifted in bliss; arms slowly sink as she/he returns to the initial pose, offering the camera a soft closed-lip smile

## 小丸子风格表情 (Maruko-chan Style Expressions)
**适用场景**: 短发波波头+齐刘海角色（与小丸子发型相似）

### M1. 小丸子生气脸 (Maruko Angry Face)
**中文**: 小丸子式的搞怪表情——标志性的锯齿状波浪刘海轮廓紧贴额头，两道夸张的倒八字浓眉高高挑起，圆溜溜的大白眼珠向内对视（斗鸡眼），两颊鼓成粉红色圆球，嘴巴缩成一个小小的螺旋状嘟嘴——但真实的头发和眼镜框架依然保留在原位
**English**: Chibi Maruko-chan–style funny expression — the iconic zigzag wavy bang outline pressed flat against her/his forehead, two exaggerated inverted-V thick eyebrows arched high, round white eyeballs crossed inward (cross-eyed), both cheeks puffed into pink spheres, mouth shrunk into a tiny spiral pout — but her/his real hair and glasses frame remain in place

### M2. 小丸子哭脸 (Maruko Crying Face)
**中文**: 小丸子式的哭脸表情——眉毛变成两条倒挂的弧线，眼睛眯成两条缝挤出两滴夸张的卡通泪珠，嘴巴大张成一个倒三角形嚎啕大哭，脸两侧各有三道汗珠飞溅线
**English**: Maruko-chan–style crying face — eyebrows curving into two inverted arcs, eyes squeezed into slits with two exaggerated cartoon teardrops flying out, mouth gaping open into an inverted triangle wailing shape, three sweat-drop motion lines on each side of the face

### M3. 小丸子惊喜脸 (Maruko Surprise Face)
**中文**: 小丸子式的惊喜脸——眉毛高高扬起呈两条弯月形，眼睛睁得滚圆闪着星星状高光，嘴巴张成一个小圆"O"型，两颊泛出兴奋的红晕，额角还飘出三条表示激动的放射线
**English**: Maruko-chan–style surprise face — eyebrows raised into two crescent arcs, eyes wide open with star-shaped highlights, mouth forming a small round "O", cheeks flushed with excitement, three radiating surprise lines floating at her/his temple

### M4. 小丸子惊恐脸 (Maruko Terrified Face)
**中文**: 小丸子式的惊恐脸——脸被纵向拉长变形，两只巨大的椭圆白眼珠几乎占满上半张脸，眉毛飞到发际线以上，嘴巴缩成一个颤抖的小点，身体两侧飘出表示惊吓的速度线
**English**: Maruko-chan–style terrified face — face vertically stretched and distorted, two enormous oval white eyeballs occupying nearly the entire upper half of the face, eyebrows flying up past the hairline, mouth shrunk into a trembling small dot, speed lines indicating shock radiating from both sides of the body

### M5. 小丸子得意脸 (Maruko Smug Face)
**中文**: 小丸子式的得意脸——眉毛一边高一边低呈挑衅状，一只眼睛睁开一只眼睛眯起（眨眼），嘴角歪向一边露出得意的坏笑，两颊各有一个小酒窝
**English**: Maruko-chan–style smug face — one eyebrow raised and one lowered in a taunting pose, one eye open and one eye squinted (winking), mouth skewed to one side in a cocky grin, small dimples on both cheeks

### M6. 小丸子偷笑脸 (Maruko Snickering Face)
**中文**: 小丸子式的偷笑脸——眉毛弯成两道月牙，眼睛眯成两条开心的弧线，嘴角咧到耳根，两颊各有一个小漩涡酒窝
**English**: Maruko-chan–style snickering face — eyebrows curved into two crescent moons, eyes narrowed into two happy arcs, grinning mouth stretching ear to ear, small spiral dimples on both cheeks

## 表情选择指南

| 角色类型 | 推荐表情组合 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 短发波波头+齐刘海 | M1→M2→M3→M4→M5（小丸子全套） | 发型与小丸子高度相似，效果最佳 |
| 长发女性 | 1→2→3→4→5（通用卡通） | 通用卡通大头 + 拉嘴/拉脸变体 |
| 短发男性 | 1→2→3→4→5（通用卡通） | 通用卡通大头 + 男性化动作 |
| 西装/正式着装 | 1→2→3→4→5 + 衣物摩擦细节 | 强调西装/正式服装与搞怪的反差 |
| 动漫角色 | 根据角色风格调整卡通脸 | 保留角色画风特征 |
